🔍 What is mvt?

mvt, short for Mobile Verification Toolkit, is a powerful yet easy-to-use tool that helps you check your Android or iOS smartphone for signs of hacking, spyware, or other security threats. Think of it as a complete security scanner for your phone that can reveal if someone has secretly gained access to your device.

Whether you're worried about your personal privacy, a journalist protecting sources, or just a regular user who wants peace of mind, mvt gives you the power to examine your device's health and spot anything suspicious.

2 Prepare Your Phone

Before running mvt, you'll need to prepare your device:

  • For Android: Enable Developer Mode and USB Debugging in your phone settings
  • For iOS: Create a backup of your iPhone using iTunes or Finder

Don't worry - this is standard for any security tool and doesn't put your data at risk.

3 Run the Scan

Launch mvt, connect your phone (or load your backup), and click "Start Scan." The tool will analyze your device and provide a clear report of anything suspicious it finds.

The process typically takes 5-15 minutes depending on your device's size and the amount of data to check.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is mvt safe to use on my phone?

Absolutely! mvt only reads data from your device and never modifies or deletes anything. It's completely read-only and safe for all users.

How long does a scan take?

Most scans complete in 10-20 minutes, but large phones with many apps might take up to 45 minutes. You can use your computer during the scan.

What if mvt finds something?

mvt provides clear instructions on what was found and recommendations for next steps. You can also consult the detailed documentation on the GitHub page for specific guidance.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No! While mvt is also used by security professionals, it's designed to be accessible for everyday users. The interface guides you through each step.

⚠️ Important Note

While mvt is extremely thorough, no security tool is 100% infallible. For maximum protection, keep your phone's operating system updated and only install apps from official stores.
